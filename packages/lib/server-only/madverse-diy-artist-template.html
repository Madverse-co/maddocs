IV. Payment of Net Revenues

1. Madverse shall pay the Owner "Net Revenues" which as used herein shall mean and include all monies actually received by Madverse, including India Royalties (defined below), which is directly and identifiably attributable to the exploitation of the Works, across the Territory, after deduction only of:

a) VAT (if any), any applicable foreign exchange rates/fees, duties, and any other taxes properly required to be deducted in any part of the Territory under applicable laws;

b) Any transaction or processing charges levied by any banks;

2. For clarity, all monies actually received by Madverse in connection with the Works are received after applicable deductions levied by the Sub-Administrator or any applicable PROs or copyright societies, at source.

3. Madverse may further charge the Owner any additional fees for any add-on features/services that are specifically opted by the Owner on the Madverse platform, including without limitation, any registration or membership fees/costs for any applicable PROs or copyright societies. Madverse shall, in its sole discretion, be entitled to charge such fees to the Owner upfront, at the time of registering for Services hereunder (on the Madverse platform) or deduct such applicable amounts, at actuals, from the Net Revenues paid to the Owner hereunder.

4. Madverse shall pay the Owner the Net Revenues actually received in the following manner:

a) Print: Eighty percent (80%) of the Net Revenues which are attributable to the reproduction/representation of the musical score and/or lyrics of the Works as physical sheet music, in books/magazines or in digital reproduction services where such exploitation takes place/originates in the Territory.

b) Mechanical royalties: In connection with the manufacture of mechanical devices (whether embodying sound alone or sound synchronized with visual images) for sale to the public and/or from the sale, lease, grant or other disposition of mechanical, electrical, digital or electronic reproduction (including by means of so called downloading, streaming, ring tones or otherwise) to the public, to the extent that royalties arising therefrom are attributable to the mechanical element therefrom, eighty percent (80%) of Net Revenues where such royalties are attributable to exploitation taking place in the Territory.

c) Synchronization royalties: In respect of the use and synchronization of the Works in connection with any audio-visual media, where such royalties arise from licenses granted in respect of productions originating in the Territory, seventy-five percent (75%) of Net Revenues.

d) Any other income (excluding performance royalties): Eighty percent (80%) of Net Revenues (other than performance royalties) received directly and identifiably from any other source whatsoever from use and/or exploitation of the Works, where such royalties are attributable to exploitation taking place in the Territory.

e) Performance royalties: The so-called "writer's share" shall be fifty percent (50%) and the so-called "publisher's share" shall be fifty percent (50%) (it being understood that if Madverse receives such writer's share in any case, the same will be paid either to the Owner or to applicable rights holders in accordance with industry practice and applicable laws of the relevant Territory). Madverse shall receive the publisher's share and shall pay the Owner eighty percent (80%) of such share hereunder where such monies are attributable to exploitation taking place in the Territory.

f) "India Royalties" shall mean the publisher's share of royalties which is 50% of publishing royalties collected by the Indian Performing Rights Society (IPRS), solely for the territory of India, and paid out to Madverse. For the sake of clarity, the writer's share of publishing royalties shall be collected directly by the applicable rights holders from IPRS. Madverse shall pay out 80% of the India Royalties in the following manner:

For instance, where the Owner is an individual author/composer, if the total royalties collected by IPRS for a particular Work is INR 100/-, the Owner shall directly receive INR 50/- from IPRS (on the writers side). Madverse shall directly receive INR 50/- from IPRS (on the publisher's side) and pay INR 30/- to the Owner and retain INR 20/-.

For instance, where the Owner is an individual label/publisher, if the total royalties collected by IPRS for a particular Work is INR 100/-, the authors and composers associated with the Work shall directly receive INR 50/- from IPRS (on the writers side). Madverse shall directly receive INR 50/- from IPRS (on the publisher's side) and pay INR 30/- to the Owner and retain INR 20/-.

5. In the event that Madverse does not receive any India Royalties from IPRS, for any reason whatsoever, including without limitation, due to any operational changes, administration changes, changes in law, rules, regulations etc. then Madverse shall be entitled to raise invoices to the Owner for such amounts (in the manner specified under Clause 4(f)) and the Owner shall be liable to pay the same within seven (7) days of date of invoice by Madverse. In the event that the Owner fails or delays any payments of such invoice amount, Madverse shall be entitled to set-off such amounts from any Net Revenues received from the rest of the Territory (excluding India).
